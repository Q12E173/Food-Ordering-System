About Us

About QikiQ Sushi

Welcome to QikiQ Sushi, where we bring you the finest sushi experience. Founded in 2024 by a group of passionate individuals from Malaysia, we are a team of four young women dedicated to providing a delightful dining experience that celebrates the art of sushi.

Our Story

QikiQ Sushi was founded in 2024 by Ng Le Qian, Tan Jia Hui, Ang Ping, and Lim Kai Qing. We started our journey with a passion for sushi and a commitment to quality and innovation. As a team of young women from Malaysia, we have grown into a beloved sushi destination through our dedication and hard work.

Mission and Vision

Our mission is to bring joy to our customers through delicious sushi and exceptional service. We envision a future where QikiQ Sushi is synonymous with quality and innovation in sushi cuisine, making it a favorite spot for sushi lovers.

Our Values and Philosophy

We believe in using the freshest ingredients, traditional techniques, and innovative flavors to create unforgettable sushi experiences. Our values are centered around quality, customer satisfaction, and sustainability. As young entrepreneurs, we strive to make a positive impact on the community and the environment.
